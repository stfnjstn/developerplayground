## Level Pack Screen

![Overview](/assets/ETMEditor/Overview.jpg)

This is the main screen of a level pack. At the top you can change the pack's **file name** and its **name** (the name is what players see in the game).

![Overview Buttons](/assets/ETMEditor/Share0.jpg)

These 4 buttons trigger:
1. Checking your level pack for errors
2. Export / share your level pack
3. Save your changes
4. Discard your changes
5. Open the about page

The **<** button on the left returns to the [start page](/ETMEditorTutorials/ETMEditorTutorial_StartDialog) and asks whether to save your changes.
