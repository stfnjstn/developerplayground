## Sharing Your Level Packs

Once you've created an amazing dungeon experience, you can share it with other players! The Level Editor provides several ways to distribute your custom level packs.

## Export Your Level Pack

![Eport](/assets/ETMEditor/Share0.jpg)

To export and share your level pack:

1. Open your level pack in the editor
2. Click the **Export** button (the share icon, second in the top toolbar). The pack is saved and checked first; a pack with errors is not exported — run the validation (the checkmark button) to see what needs fixing
3. The share sheet opens with a `.etdm` file containing all your levels, monsters, items, and settings — choose where to send or save it

## Sharing Methods

![Share](/assets/ETMEditor/Share1.jpg)

You can share your level pack using:

- **The Dungeon - Get Out**: if the game is installed on the same device, it appears right in the share sheet — tap it and the pack is imported into the game immediately
- **Email** or **Messages**: Send the `.etdm` file directly to friends
- **Local or iCloud Storage**: Save to Files app for later access
- **AirDrop**: Share directly to nearby devices

### Opening Shared Level Packs

When someone receives your `.etdm` file:

![Play](/assets/ETMEditor/Share2.jpg)

1. Tap the `.etdm` file — in the Files app, in Mail, in Messages or wherever it arrived. "The Dungeon - Get Out" opens with it directly
2. The game imports the level pack into its **imported** folder
3. When you start a new game, a list of level packs comes up: the built-in dungeon on top, then a section **Imported** with every pack you have received. Pick the one you want to play
4. To remove a pack you no longer need, swipe its row to the left in that list

Players can also save the `.etdm` file and import it later by opening it whenever they're ready.

### Level packs from the built-in editor

If you build your levels with the editor built into the game, there is nothing to export: the packs you save there show up in the new-game list under **Level Editor**, next to the imported ones, and always play in the state you last saved them. Export is only needed to pass a pack on to someone else.

### Export to AppleTV or AppleWatch

Unfortunately both platforms have no files app or Airdrop. Therefore deploying is a little bit more complex: 

1. Import the levelpack on Mac, iPad or Iphone as described above.
2. Start a new game for your levelpack
3. Save the game and choose cloud as target (the switch top right)
4. Open the game on AppleTV or Watch
5. Load the game from cloud target

### Import into editor

![Editor Import](/assets/ETMEditor/ImportLevelPack.jpg)

You can also import a level pack into the level editor: touch **Import Level Packs** on the start page and choose the file in the Files app. The pack then appears under *Recent Level Packs*.
