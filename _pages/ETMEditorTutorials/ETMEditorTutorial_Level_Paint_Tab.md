![Detail Select Tile](/assets/ETMEditor/DetailPaint.jpg)

At the top of the tile chooser on the right side you see the currently selected tile type. Simply tap a tile in the drawing area to replace it with the selected type — or **drag across the map** to paint several tiles in one stroke. While Paint Mode is active the map does not scroll; switch to another mode to scroll it.

Be careful: painting a tile overwrites its settings, monsters, game items, triggers & actions and textures. Placing a **Start** tile moves the player's starting position there.
Use the revert button (✕) in the header if you accidentally overwrote something, and the save button to store intermediate results.
